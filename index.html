Mobile Booking Application — UX Case Study & Step-by-Step Rationale

Themes:
Step 1 Salon Business

Choose a Service-Based Business

We selected **"Aura Glow — Luxury Salon & Spa"** to showcase elite aesthetic capabilities. High-end personal care applications require extreme trust, premium styling, and clean interfaces that reflect the real-world treatment atmosphere.

Premium Aesthetics build trust for high-value bookings.
Step 2 Requirements Analysis

Understand User Requirements

User research indicates booking drop-offs occur due to confusing scheduling flow and complex profile management. Users need a high-speed calendar overview, distinct pricing structures, and quick login persistence.

Sophia, 28

Corporate Exec

Needs one-click slots, calendar sync, and stylist notes.

Liam, 34

Regular Groomer

Prioritizes history re-booking, loyalty stats, and push notifications.

Step 3 Scope & Features

Define App Features

We scoped features tailored to mobile usability: simple login, immediate category filtering, a clean dynamic monthly calendar widget, instant stylist assignment, digital wallet passes, and customizable appointment logs.

  • High-fidelity booking workflow within 4 steps
  • Stylist profiles with specialist experience badges
  • Automated local receipt logging
Step 4 User Flow Planning

Create User Flow Diagram

The flow minimizes friction. Users are onboarded, authorized, routed to a highly indexed dashboard, shown a detailed summary drawer, given a single-screen scheduler, and issued a digital pass.

Splash Onboarding Auth Gate Dashboard Details Scheduler Receipt
Step 5 Wireframing

Sketch Low-Fidelity Wireframes

We lay out skeletal components focusing purely on visual hierarchy and touch targets before introducing aesthetic elements.

Interactive skeletal overlay active

Step 6 App Onboarding

Design Onboarding Screens

Designed 3 high-impact onboarding frames highlighting brand values (Luxury treatments, seamless scheduling, custom credit balances). Simple illustrations and indicators motivate progression.

Microtip: Carousel dots shift size dynamically to indicate active states.
Step 7 Account Gateway

Design Login & Signup Screen

Combines forms into a single viewport with a fast slide-tab. Large inputs with left-aligned descriptive icons, autocomplete capability, and social sign-in helpers keep authorization fast.

Step 8 Main Dashboard

Create Home Screen UI

A comprehensive, clean hub containing a personalized greetings banner, custom-filtered treatment category badges, popular items, and an active confirmation ticker.

Confirmed bookings appear dynamically at the top of the feed to allow speed rescheduling!
Step 9 Treatment Detail

Design Service Selection Screen

Focuses heavily on imagery and explicit price plans. Provides transparent specs (duration, reviews, special location) and stylist selection profiles in a horizontally scrollable drawer.

Step 10 Slots Scheduling

Design Date & Time Slot Booking

This is the core booking screen. Utilizes an intuitive calendar grid and grouped slot buckets (Morning/Afternoon/Evening) with distinct active, empty, and filled styles to prevent cognitive fatigue.

Step 11 Ticket Confirmation

Create Appointment Confirmation Screen

Features an elegant ticket pass with custom dashed separator, generated secure transaction ID, clear time details, and a success checkmark animation with custom calendar action.

Step 12 Account & History

Design Profile & Settings Screen

Shows active loyalty details, lists past and upcoming bookings dynamically parsed from local storage, and includes profile configuration toggles.

Step 13 Design Tokens

Choose Colors & Typography

Step 18 Design Rationale

UX Design Rationale

Every choice aligns with established mobile usability paradigms to maximize conversions and satisfaction.

1. Consistent Primary Buttons: Large tap targets prevent missed selections and visual clutter on small viewports.

2. Progressive Onboarding: Engaging slide cards reduce initial user anxiety and increase registration conversions.

3. Status-Alert Ticker: Pinning the upcoming appointment directly on the dashboard gives users immediate confirmation, reducing anxiety.
